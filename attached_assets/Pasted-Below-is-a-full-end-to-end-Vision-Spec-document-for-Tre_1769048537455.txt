# TREASURE COAST AI — VISION SPEC (Full Operating Document)

## 1) The Big Promise

Treasure Coast AI is a **premium, universal, multi-tenant chatbot SaaS** for local businesses.

A business owner’s experience should feel like:

* **“This thing is making me money.”**
* **“It feels like the smartest assistant I’ve ever seen.”**
* **“I don’t have to think about it.”**
* **“I can see leads, bookings, and what customers want.”**

Your experience as the agency owner should feel like:

* **“I can onboard a business fast.”**
* **“I enter basic business info.”**
* **“AI does the heavy lifting (drafts, structure, suggestions).”**
* **“I approve — nothing goes live without review.”**
* **“Every client is isolated, secure, and scalable.”**

This is built to be **a must-have**: once a business sees it, they understand the value immediately.

---

## 2) Product Identity and Positioning

### Core Positioning

* Not “a chatbot.”
* A **Business Growth Assistant**:

  * captures leads,
  * routes bookings,
  * answers accurately from verified business data,
  * shows business owners proof it’s working.

### Universal Core, Templates Provide the Magic

* **No single industry baked into the core platform.**
* The core is universal: organizations, bots, KB, services, hours, booking flow, analytics, leads.
* Each industry is powered by:

  * **templates** (starter KB / FAQs / intent tuning),
  * **AI suggestions** (drafts),
  * ongoing improvement from better templates + structured onboarding.

---

## 3) The “Premium Agency-Built” Standard

When someone lands on the site or sees the dashboard, it must look like:

* **Multi-million-dollar agency work**
* clean and modern,
* perfect typography & spacing,
* polished empty states,
* responsive and fast,
* micro-interactions that feel expensive (subtle, not gimmicky),
* consistent UI components across marketing + app + widget.

“Cheap SaaS vibes” is failure.

---

## 4) The Two Main User Types

### A) Agency (You)

Roles:

* **AGENCY_OWNER** (full control)
* **AGENCY_ADMIN** (almost full control)

Agency responsibilities:

* create organizations and bots
* invite client users
* configure brand, services, hours, contact info
* create/approve KB content
* optionally use AI to generate drafts, then approve

Agency should be able to set up a new client in minutes.

### B) Client (Business Owner / Staff)

Role:

* **CLIENT**

Client experience:

* logs in, sees dashboards (proof of value)
* views leads, analytics, conversations/topics
* can edit only lightweight business settings **if** enabled (allowClientEdits)
* never sees agency-level secrets
* never has the power to break anything critical

---

## 5) Non-Negotiable Rules (Guardrails)

### Truth Mode (No Hallucinations)

* The assistant must answer **only from verified sources**:

  * published KB entries
  * business settings (hours, services, contact, policies)
* If it doesn’t know, it says so clearly and does one of:

  * offers to capture lead,
  * offers booking flow,
  * asks for the missing detail and suggests adding it to settings/KB.

### Deterministic Booking Flow (Always the same flow)

When booking intent is detected:

1. Service selection
2. Name
3. Phone
4. Email
5. Confirmation + correct booking link

No random AI freelancing.

### Security + Isolation

* Tenants are **strictly isolated**.
* RBAC is enforced everywhere.
* No cross-org leaks (ever).
* Production cannot have dev bypass.

## 6) Customer Journey (How It Works in Real Life)

### Step 1: Prospect sees marketing site

* premium landing page
* simple explanation: “AI that answers, books, and captures leads”
* demo widget they can try
* request demo CTA

### Step 2: You onboard business

You either:

* create org + bot manually (fast), or
* use onboarding wizard to enter business info (still fast)

### Step 3: Business embeds widget on their website

* they paste a script snippet
* widget loads on allowed domains only
* chat starts capturing leads/booking

### Step 4: Business logs in and sees value

They open dashboard and immediately see:

* leads (with HOT/WARM/COLD)
* booking funnel performance
* clicks
* top topics customers ask
* “revenue influenced” / value indicators (where possible)
* activity timeline

They can manage leads and follow up.

## 7) What the Business Owner Must See (Client Dashboard)

The dashboard must answer:

* “Is this bringing me customers?”
* “What are people asking?”
* “Who should I call right now?”
* “What service is converting best?”

### Must-have dashboard modules

1. **Analytics Overview**

   * unique conversations
   * funnel:

     * service selected
     * lead created
     * booking link shown
     * booking link clicked
   * conversion rate
   * trends by day (7/30/90)
   * clicks by service
   * top topics

2. **Lead Inbox**

   * filters (date, status, temperature, service, search)
   * lead detail drawer:

     * contact info (tap/click call/email)
     * service selected
     * score + reasons
     * notes
     * status update buttons
     * captured answers

3. **Settings (Lightweight, safe edits)**

   * services (name, price, booking/payment URLs)
   * business hours
   * contact info
   * only editable if allowClientEdits=true (otherwise read-only + locked banner)

4. **Proof Layer**

   * timeline / last 24h activity
   * highlight hot leads
   * “money proof” language throughout (value focus)

## 9) Agency Workflow (The “Money Loop”)

The agency workflow must be:

* fast
* repeatable
* scalable

### Core agency workflow loop

1. Create Organization
2. Invite Client user(s)
3. Create Bot (choose template)
4. Fill business settings:

   * services
   * hours
   * contact info
5. AI generates drafts:

   * about text
   * FAQs
   * suggested KB entries
6. You review + approve
7. Publish bot
8. Provide embed snippet
9. Client sees leads + analytics
10. You sell next client

This loop must become “boring” (easy, repeatable, reliable).

## 10) Templates and AI Improvement (How the Platform Gets Smarter)

### What “getting better and better” means (realistic, scalable)

Not “AI magically learns from everything” in a risky way.

Instead:

* You improve:

  * templates
  * structured onboarding
  * better draft prompts
  * better KB seed packs
  * better scoring rules / conversion guidance

AI improvements happen through:

* stronger template libraries
* better data structure
* better prompts
* more complete business info
* optional website import → draft KB → review

**Everything that impacts answers must still be verified/published.**

## 11) Operational Rules

### Roles and Permissions

* Owner/Admin:

  * edit everything
  * see secret tokens (domain verification)
  * manage members/invites
* Client:

  * view analytics/leads/settings
  * edit only if allowClientEdits enabled

### Publishing Model

* Draft vs Published KB
* AI outputs are **always Draft**
* Only Published content is used by Truth Mode answers

### Logs and Audit Friendliness

* important actions create DataEvents:

  * booking milestones
  * lead creation
  * link clicks
  * major settings changes (optional but preferred)

## 14) The Hard “No” List (What We Don’t Do)

To keep it safe, credible, and premium:

* No hallucinated answers.
* No “AI just invents your services/hours/prices.”
* No auto-publishing AI content without review.
* No cross-tenant data access, ever.
* No clients managing dangerous settings by default.
* No cluttered UI, no cheap templates, no “startup toy” feel.
