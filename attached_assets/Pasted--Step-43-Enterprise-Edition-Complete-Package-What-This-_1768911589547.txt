## 📊 State Machine Flow

```
IDLE
  ↓ (booking intent detected)
SERVICE_SELECTION
  ↓ (service selected)
LEAD_NAME
  ↓ (valid name entered)
LEAD_PHONE
  ↓ (valid phone entered)  
LEAD_EMAIL
  ↓ (valid email entered)
COMPLETE
  ↓ (flow done)
IDLE (reset)
```

**Global Commands (work from any state):**

- `cancel` → Reset to IDLE
- `restart` → Back to SERVICE_SELECTION
- `back` → Previous step
